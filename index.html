Spelling Bee

How to play

Make words of at least four letters. Every word must contain the yellow center letter. Letters can be used more than once.

Four-letter words earn 1 point; longer words earn 1 point per letter. A pangram uses all seven letters and earns 7 extra points.

Type or tap the letters. Press Enter to submit, Backspace to delete, or Escape to clear.
